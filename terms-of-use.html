Blocking and reporting

  • Block user: in a chat, open the menu (three dots) and choose Block user. It ends the chat, and that person's NearbyChat installation can no longer connect with you; if they try, they are told they are blocked. Blocking recognises the person's installation, not their display name, so it still works if they change their name, and it does not affect other people who use the same name. It stops working if they reinstall NearbyChat or clear its data, because that creates a new identity. Your block list is stored only on your device and is lost if you clear NearbyChat's data. Manage blocked users with Blocked users on the home screen.
  • Report user: in a chat, open the menu and choose Report user. Choose a reason, add optional details, and optionally include the messages on screen. NearbyChat prepares the report and opens your email or sharing app; it is only sent if you send it.

How reports are handled

NearbyChat has no server, and the developer cannot see, read, or remove your conversations. The developer reviews reports it receives at the contact below and may reply to the reporter, change or restrict features in later versions of the app, and notify authorities where the law requires (for example, reporting child sexual abuse material to the appropriate authority). If someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services.
