NearbyChat Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-04 (applies to NearbyChat version 1.0)

Developer: Hanif Makakmayum
Privacy contact: ahub.support@gmail.com

What NearbyChat is

NearbyChat lets two people with nearby Android phones chat directly, device to device, using Google's Nearby Connections technology. The developer does not operate a chat server, and there are no accounts or sign-in.

What the developer collects

Nothing. The developer does not receive, collect, or have access to your name, your messages, your nearby connections, or any usage data from the app. NearbyChat contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking software, and it does not use a cloud messaging service.

Data NearbyChat stores on your device

NearbyChat keeps only these items, in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Your display name, which you choose.
  • Your theme choice (Light or Dark), if you selected one.
  • A single yes/no record that you accepted the Terms of Use.
  • The display names of users you have blocked, if any.

NearbyChat does not store chat messages, encryption or session keys, verification codes, a history of who you connected to, or any identity of the people you chat with. Android backup is turned off for the app.

Data sent to the other person

  • Your display name is broadcast to nearby devices that are searching with NearbyChat while you have the "Nearby" screen open, and is shown to the person you connect with. Anyone nearby who is running NearbyChat can see it, before any connection is made. Do not use a name you want to keep private.
  • Messages you send go directly to the person you are connected to. They are encrypted on your device before sending (an ephemeral P-256 key exchange, HKDF-SHA256, and AES-256-GCM) with a new key for every connection. Both people compare a 6-digit verification code and must both confirm it before messages can be sent. The code is never transmitted.
  • The other person can see, copy, or screenshot what you send them. We cannot prevent that.

What happens to your messages

Messages exist only in memory while a chat is open. They are removed when you leave the chat, when the connection ends, or when the app process is closed or restarted. If you rotate your phone or change a display setting, the conversation stays on screen but is still only held in memory.

Permissions and why they are needed

NearbyChat asks for the permissions Android requires for finding and connecting to nearby devices:

  • Nearby devices, Bluetooth, and Wi-Fi permissions: to discover and connect to nearby phones.
  • Location permission: on many Android versions, Android requires it for Bluetooth and Wi-Fi scanning. NearbyChat does not read, record, store, or send your location. It only checks whether the device's location and Wi-Fi scanning settings are on so it can ask you to turn them on.
  • Notifications (optional): to show a notification while a chat connection is active, so the connection can keep working when your screen is off. If you deny it, NearbyChat still works.
  • Foreground service: runs only while a chat connection is active, and stops when the chat ends or the app is removed from recent apps.

The app also declares the Internet permission. NearbyChat's own code does not send or receive anything over the Internet and has no server to contact.

Google Play services

NearbyChat uses Google Play services (Nearby Connections and Location Settings). Google's software, not the developer, carries out discovery and the device-to-device connection, and it may use Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and other radios to do so. Google may process information about your device and its nearby connections under Google's own privacy policy and terms. The developer does not control this and does not receive that information.

Reports and blocking

If you report a user, NearbyChat prepares a text report on your device: the reason you pick, any details you type, the other person's display name, the time, the app version, and, only if you tick the box, the messages currently on screen. NearbyChat does not send it. It opens the email or sharing app you choose, and it is sent only if you send it. NearbyChat does not keep a copy. If you send a report to the developer, it is used to assess the report and decide what action to take, and is kept only as long as needed for that.

Blocking is stored locally as a list of display names. See the Terms of Use for its limits.

Keeping and deleting your data

Everything NearbyChat stores is on your device only. You can change your name in the app, unblock users from the Blocked users screen, and remove all remaining data by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app. Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to delete on a server.

Children

NearbyChat is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If NearbyChat's data practices change, this policy will be updated and the date above will change.
